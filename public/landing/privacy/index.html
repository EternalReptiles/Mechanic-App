Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains how Spannr ("we", "us") handles personal information. Spannr is operated by [legal name of the business or company], ABN [ABN]. We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

Two kinds of information

1. Information about Spannr's users. This is the mechanics, workshop owners and team members who use Spannr. We're responsible for this information.

2. Information businesses keep about their own customers. This includes customer names, phone numbers, vehicles and job history. Each business decides what it records and how it uses it. We store and process it on the business's behalf to provide Spannr. If you're a customer of a business that uses Spannr, please contact that business first about your information. We'll help them respond.

What we collect

From Spannr users

  • Your name, email address and password (stored securely, never in readable form), and your role in the business.
  • Business details: business name, ABN, address, phone, email, logo, bank details for invoices, prices and settings.
  • Subscription and billing records. Card details are collected and held by Stripe, not by Spannr.
  • For card payments: your Stripe account's identifier and status. Identity checks for payments are done by Stripe.
  • Technical information needed to run the Service securely, such as sign-in times and basic device and browser details.

Entered by businesses about their customers

  • Names, phone numbers, email and street addresses.
  • Vehicle details such as make, model, registration, VIN and odometer, plus service history, inspections, quotes, invoices, payment status, notes, photos and videos.

When a customer responds to a link

When a customer opens a quote, video walk-around or sign-off link, we record when it was viewed. If they approve, decline or sign, we record the decisions, the name typed, the signature, the time, and the IP address and browser details of the device used. This is kept as evidence of what was agreed. Links expire 30 days after they're sent.

On your phone

  • Camera and microphone: used only when you take job photos or record a video walk-around.
  • Location: Tap to Pay uses your phone's location, because Apple and Stripe require it to process the payment. Spannr doesn't store or track your location.
  • Offline copy: so Spannr works without signal, your business's information is also saved on your device until it syncs.

How we use it

  • To provide Spannr: storing and syncing your information, generating documents, and sending the texts and emails you ask Spannr to send.
  • To process subscriptions and card payments through Stripe.
  • To keep Spannr secure, prevent misuse, fix problems and improve the Service.
  • To contact you about your account, billing, important changes and support.

We don't sell personal information. We don't use your customers' information for our own marketing, and Spannr doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Information is also shared with the people the Service is meant to share it with. For example, quotes and invoices go to the business's customers, and a business's information is visible to its team members. We may also disclose information when the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety or our legal rights.

Security

Information is encrypted in transit and at rest. Each business's data is kept separate, and access controls make sure users can only see their own business's information. Video walk-around and quote links use long, unguessable addresses, so anyone with a link can open it until it expires. Please only send links to the right person. No system is perfectly secure. If a data breach is likely to cause serious harm, we'll notify affected businesses and people, and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner, as the law requires.

How long we keep it

We keep information while your account is active. If a business account is closed, we keep its data for 90 days so it can come back or ask for a copy, and then delete it, except where we must keep records by law (for example, billing records for tax). Businesses can delete individual customers and jobs at any time.

Access, correction and complaints

You can see and update most of your information in Spannr. To ask for a copy of your personal information, ask for a correction, or make a privacy complaint, email hello@spannr.au. We'll respond within 30 days. If you're not happy with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Children

Spannr is for businesses and isn't intended for people under 18.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always at spannr.au/privacy. If a change is significant, we'll let users know.
