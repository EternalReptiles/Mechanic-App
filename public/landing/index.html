Questions

Do I need a credit card to try it?

No. Sign up and you get 30 days with every feature unlocked. Choose a plan only if you want to keep going.

Does it work on iPhone, Android and computers?

Yes. Spannr works in the web browser on any phone, tablet or computer, and you can add it to your home screen like an app. Workshops get a layout made for desktop screens, and an iPhone app with Tap to Pay is on the way.

Do my customers need to download anything?

No. Quotes, video walk-arounds, sign-offs and invoices open from a link in a text or email, on any phone.

Is it per user?

No. You pay one price for your business. Each plan includes a set number of users (unlimited on Business), so adding a technician doesn't add to your bill.

How do card payments work?

You connect your own Stripe account in a few minutes, and payments go straight to your bank. Stripe charges its standard card fees and Spannr adds 0.5%. There are no monthly terminal fees.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. There's no lock-in contract. Cancel from your billing settings and you keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.
